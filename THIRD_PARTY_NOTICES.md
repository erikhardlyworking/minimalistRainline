# Third-party material

Rainline's own application source and vector artwork are covered by the root
MIT licence. Its debug APK contains no third-party runtime dependencies.

The generated Gradle wrapper scripts and JAR retain Gradle's licensing. Copies
of the distribution's [licence](licenses/gradle-LICENSE.txt) and
[notice](licenses/gradle-NOTICE.txt) are included with the source.

The build downloads Android build tools; local JVM tests download JUnit,
Hamcrest and JSON-java under their respective licences. These are build/test
dependencies and are not packaged in the Rainline APK.

Weather data is separately attributed to MET Norway under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). See the in-app
Weather data page and [MET's licensing information](https://docs.api.met.no/doc/License).
